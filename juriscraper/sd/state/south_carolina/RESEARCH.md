# South Carolina Courts Research

## Court Structure

South Carolina has a unified judicial system administered by the Chief Justice, consisting of two appellate courts and multiple trial courts.

### 1. Supreme Court of South Carolina
- **Website**: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/supreme-court/
- Highest court in South Carolina
- 5 Justices (1 Chief Justice + 4 Associate Justices)
- Justices are elected by the General Assembly for 10-year terms
- Terms are staggered; justices may be reelected to any number of terms
- Candidates must be screened by the Judicial Merit Selection Commission
- Location: 1231 Gervais Street, Columbia, SC 29201
- Phone: (803) 734-1080

#### Jurisdiction
- Exclusive appellate jurisdiction for:
  - Appeals involving a sentence of death
  - Public Service Commission rate decisions
  - Constitutional challenges to state statutes or local ordinances
  - Public bonded indebtedness judgments
  - Election-related judgments
  - State Grand Jury investigation orders
  - Family court orders relating to abortion by a minor
- May certify appeals from Court of Appeals for its own decision
- Reviews certiorari petitions from Court of Appeals decisions
- Reviews post-conviction relief actions by writ of certiorari
- Original jurisdiction for mandamus, certiorari, and other extraordinary writs
- May answer certified questions of law from federal courts or other state appellate courts

### 2. Court of Appeals
- **Website**: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/court-of-appeals/
- Intermediate appellate court (newest court in system, commenced September 1, 1983)
- 9 Judges (1 Chief Judge + 8 Associate Judges)
- Judges are elected by the General Assembly for 6-year terms
- Terms are staggered
- Candidates must be screened by the Judicial Merit Selection Commission
- Location: The Calhoun Building, 1220 Senate Street, Columbia, SC 29201
- Phone: (803) 734-1890
- Email: ctappfilings@sccourts.org

#### Jurisdiction
- Handles nearly all direct appeals from:
  - Circuit courts
  - Family courts
  - Administrative Law Court
  - Workers' Compensation Commission
  - Other state courts and tribunals as set forth by statute
- Sits as three panels of three judges each or as a whole
- May hear oral arguments and motions in any county

### 3. Circuit Court (Trial Court)
- **Website**: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/trial-courts/circuit-court/
- General jurisdiction trial court
- Court of Common Pleas (civil) and Court of General Sessions (criminal)

### 4. Family Court (Trial Court)
- **Website**: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/trial-courts/family-court/
- Handles domestic relations, juvenile matters
- Has separate case management system (FCCMS)

### 5. Probate Court (Trial Court)
- **Website**: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/trial-courts/probate-court/
- Handles estates, guardianships, conservatorships

### 6. Magistrate Court (Trial Court)
- **Website**: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/trial-courts/magistrate-court/
- Limited jurisdiction court

### 7. Municipal Court (Trial Court)
- **Website**: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/trial-courts/municipal-court/
- City/town level courts

### 8. Master-In-Equity Court (Trial Court)
- **Website**: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/trial-courts/master-in-equity-court/
- Hears equity matters referred by Circuit Court

### 9. Treatment Court (Specialized)
- **Website**: https://www.sccourts.org/courts/trial-courts/treatment-court/home/
- Drug courts and other treatment-focused courts

## Opinion Types and Document Categories

### Published vs. Unpublished Opinions
South Carolina distinguishes between:
1. **Published Opinions** - Precedential decisions
2. **Unpublished Opinions** - Non-precedential decisions

Both Supreme Court and Court of Appeals issue both types.

### Advance Sheets
- Weekly compilation of appellate court opinions
- Available from November 25, 2000 to present
- Published in PDF format
- Contains both Supreme Court and Court of Appeals opinions

### Court Orders Types
- Administrative Orders
- Supreme Court Orders
- Judicial/Legal Conduct Orders
- Court Rule Maintenance Orders
- Aiken v. Byars Judicial Appointments

## Judicial Selection Process

South Carolina has a unique judicial selection process:

1. **Judicial Merit Selection Commission** - Screens all candidates for appellate courts
2. **General Assembly Election** - Joint public vote elects judges
3. Candidates can be from any geographical region in the state
4. Terms:
   - Supreme Court Justices: 10 years
   - Court of Appeals Judges: 6 years

## South Carolina Judicial Circuit System

South Carolina is divided into 16 judicial circuits for trial court purposes. The state has 46 counties, each with its own:
- Clerk of Court
- Register of Deeds
- Probate Judge
- Magistrate Courts
- Municipal Courts (where applicable)

## Notes

1. **Opinion Numbering**: Supreme Court opinions are in the 28,000+ range; Court of Appeals opinions are in the 6,000+ range.

2. **Unified Judicial System**: All courts operate under the administrative supervision of the Chief Justice.

3. **General Assembly Election**: Unlike many states, South Carolina judges are elected by the legislature rather than by popular vote or gubernatorial appointment.

4. **Modern Website**: The website was recently redesigned with improved search features, easy access to court records, county-specific contact pages, and Spanish translation options.

5. **PDF-Based Documents**: All opinions, orders, and advance sheets are provided as PDF downloads.

6. **Multiple Case Management Systems**:
   - C-Track for appellate courts
   - Public Index for trial courts
   - FCCMS for Family Court

7. **Email Notifications Available**: Users can subscribe to receive notifications when items of interest are posted.

8. **AI Policy**: South Carolina has an Interim Policy on the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence (Order 2025-03-25-01).

9. **Privacy Protection**: Strict requirements for redacting personal identifying information in court filings.

10. **Historic Data Limitation**: C-Track data generally only available for cases filed on or after May 1, 2012.
