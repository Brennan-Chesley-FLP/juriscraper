# Washington Courts Research

## Court Structure

Washington has a unified court system with two appellate courts at the apex.

### 1. Supreme Court of Washington
- **Website**: https://www.courts.wa.gov/appellate_trial_courts/SupremeCourt/?fa=supremecourt.welcome
- Highest court in Washington (court of last resort)
- 9 Justices elected to 6-year staggered terms
- Location: Temple of Justice, Capitol Campus, Olympia
- Chief Justice: Debra L. Stephens

#### Jurisdiction
- Final appellate jurisdiction in all matters
- Original jurisdiction in habeas corpus and other writs
- Reviews petitions for review from Court of Appeals
- Administers the unified court system

### 2. Court of Appeals of Washington
- **Website**: https://www.courts.wa.gov/appellate_trial_courts/
- Intermediate appellate court established in 1969
- Three geographic divisions:
  - **Division I** (Seattle): King, Snohomish, Skagit, Whatcom, Island, San Juan counties
  - **Division II** (Tacoma): Pierce, Kitsap, Clallam, Jefferson, Mason, Thurston, Grays Harbor, Lewis, Pacific, Wahkiakum, Cowlitz, Clark, Skamania, Klickitat counties
  - **Division III** (Spokane): Spokane, Adams, Asotin, Benton, Chelan, Columbia, Douglas, Ferry, Franklin, Garfield, Grant, Kittitas, Lincoln, Okanogan, Pend Oreille, Stevens, Walla Walla, Whitman, Yakima counties

### 3. Trial Courts
- **Superior Courts**: General jurisdiction (39 counties)
- **District Courts**: Limited jurisdiction
- **Municipal Courts**: City ordinance violations

## Opinion Types and Document Categories

### Supreme Court
- **Published Opinions**: Full written opinions with precedential value
- **Orders**: Administrative and procedural orders
- **Slip Opinions**: Filed on decision day, may be revised before final publication

### Court of Appeals
- **Published Opinions**: Precedential value under RCW 2.06.040
- **Unpublished Opinions**: No precedential value; may not be cited as binding authority under GR 14.1
- **Opinions Published in Part**: Some portions published, others not

## Case Number Formats

### Supreme Court
- Format: `XXX,XXX-X` (e.g., `103,469-5`)
- PDF filename: Remove commas (e.g., `1034695.pdf`)

### Court of Appeals
- Format: `XXXXX-X` (5 digits, dash, single digit)
- Division indicated by Roman numeral suffix (I, II, III)
- Examples:
  - Division I: `86711-3-I`
  - Division II: `59813-2-II`
  - Division III: `40341-6-III`

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Website Architecture
- **Primary Domain**: www.courts.wa.gov
- **Case Search**: dw.courts.wa.gov
- **Appellate Documents**: acdocportal.courts.wa.gov
- **E-Filing**: ac.courts.wa.gov
- **Official Reports**: lexisnexis.com/clients/wareports/

### Opinion Pages Structure
- Recent opinions page shows Supreme Court and Court of Appeals separately
- Court of Appeals further separated into:
  - Published opinions
  - Unpublished opinions
  - Opinions published in part
- Each entry contains:
  - File date
  - Case number (linked to info sheet)
  - PDF icon (linked to opinion file)
  - Division (for COA)
  - Case title
  - File contents description

### Opinion Info Sheet Contains
- Docket number
- Case title
- File date
- Oral argument date
- Source of appeal (lower court info)
- All justices/judges with their roles
- Counsel of record with addresses

### Opinion Search Form
- Court level filter (All, Supreme Court Only, Court of Appeals Only)
- Publication status filter (All, Published Only, Unpublished Only)
- Date range (begin/end)
- Keywords/phrase search
- Search type options (phrase, all words, any words, advanced)

### JavaScript Requirements
- Main opinion pages work without JavaScript
- Case search portal requires JavaScript
- E-filing portal requires JavaScript

### PDF Format
- Opinions provided as Adobe Acrobat PDF documents
- Direct download via URL patterns

### Existing Scrapers
Washington already has working scrapers in juriscraper:
- Check `juriscraper/opinions/united_states/state/` for existing Washington scrapers

## Notes

1. **Slip Opinions vs. Official Reports**: Opinions on the courts.wa.gov site are slip opinions that may be revised before final publication in the official Washington Reports 2d and Washington Appellate Reports.

2. **Unpublished Opinions**: Under RCW 2.06.040 and GR 14.1, Court of Appeals unpublished opinions lack precedential value and may not be cited as binding authority.

3. **Three Divisions**: The Court of Appeals is geographically divided into three divisions covering different parts of the state.

4. **TVW Coverage**: Oral arguments are broadcast live and archived by TVW, Washington's Public Affairs Network, dating back to 1996.

5. **Colleen M. Melody**: New Justice sworn in January 21, 2026.

6. **Electronic Filing**: Available for appellate courts via ac.courts.wa.gov.

7. **Case Search Updates**: Data on dw.courts.wa.gov is updated within 24 hours of clerk entry.

8. **50th Anniversary**: The Court of Appeals is celebrating 50 years (established 1969).

9. **Reporter of Decisions**: Maintains official opinions; content errors can be reported to reporter@courts.wa.gov.

10. **LexisNexis Free Access**: Official published opinions available free at lexisnexis.com/clients/wareports/.
