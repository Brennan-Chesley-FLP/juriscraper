# Mississippi Courts Research

## Court Structure

Mississippi has a two-tier appellate court system:

### 1. Mississippi Supreme Court (SCT)
- **Website**: https://courts.ms.gov/appellatecourts/sc/sc.php
- Highest court in Mississippi (court of last resort)
- 9 Justices elected from 3 districts (3 positions per district)
- Justices serve 8-year terms (non-partisan elections)
- Each justice participates in deciding appeals from the entire state
- Decisions are by majority vote
- Direct appeals include: annexations, bond issues, constitutionality challenges, death penalty cases, disciplinary matters, election contests, certified questions from federal court, utility rates, cases of first impression, and issues of broad public interest
- Term/Sittings: 6 per year (First through Sixth Sitting)

### 2. Mississippi Court of Appeals (COA)
- **Website**: https://courts.ms.gov/appellatecourts/coa/coa.php
- Intermediate appellate court (error correction court)
- 10 Judges elected from 5 districts (2 positions per district)
- Judges serve 8-year terms (non-partisan elections)
- Created by Legislature in 1995 to relieve Supreme Court backlog
- Hears cases assigned by the Supreme Court
- Issues settled law/disputed facts cases
- Supreme Court may review COA decisions; if declined, COA decision stands

### Trial Courts (for reference)
- **Circuit Courts**: 23 districts, 57 judges - felony criminal and civil lawsuits
- **Chancery Courts**: 20 districts, 52 judges - equity, domestic, guardianships, wills
- **County Courts**: 24 courts, 34 judges - eminent domain, juvenile, some civil
- **Justice Courts**: 82 courts, 198 judges - small claims, misdemeanors, traffic
- **Municipal Courts**: 239 courts - misdemeanors, ordinances, traffic
- **Youth Courts**: Handled by County Court judges or Chancery judges/referees
- **Intervention Courts**: 44 drug intervention + 6 mental health treatment courts

## Case Number Format

Mississippi appellate case numbers follow the pattern: `{YYYY}-{TYPE}-{NUMBER}-{COURT}`

### Case Type Codes
- **CA**: Civil Appeal
- **KA**: Criminal Appeal (Felony)
- **SA**: Supreme Court Assignment / Special Assignment
- **CT**: Certiorari
- **FC**: Federal Court (Certified Question)
- **WC**: Workers' Compensation

### Court Suffixes
- **SCT**: Supreme Court
- **COA**: Court of Appeals

### Examples
- `2024-KA-01001-SCT` - Criminal appeal to Supreme Court
- `2025-SA-00614-SCT` - Special assignment to Supreme Court
- `2025-WC-00046-COA` - Workers' compensation appeal to Court of Appeals
- `2024-CA-01277-SCT` - Civil appeal to Supreme Court
- `2023-CT-01245-SCT` - Certiorari petition to Supreme Court

## Opinion Types and Publication

### Hand Down Lists
- Decisions are released on "Hand Down Lists" on specific dates (typically Wednesdays)
- Available back to 1996 for both courts
- Calendar picker interface with clickable dates that have decisions

### Opinion Content on Hand Down Lists
Each opinion entry includes:
- Author (Justice/Judge name)
- Case number (linked to PDF)
- Case name and parties
- Lower court and case number
- Ruling date from lower court
- Ruling judge
- Disposition (Affirmed, Reversed, etc.)
- Vote breakdown (which justices concur/dissent)

### Publication Status
- Opinions marked with "X" appear to be published
- EN BANC decisions indicate full court participation

### Docket Number Format
- Opinion IDs: 6-digit sequential numbers (e.g., `CO189869`)
- Lower Court Case Numbers: Format like `53CI1:23-cr-00051-K-1`

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Website Structure
- **Domain**: courts.ms.gov
- PHP-based website
- Uses jQuery and jQuery Migrate
- Calendar widget for date selection on decisions pages
- reCAPTCHA protection on some pages

### Scraping Approach
1. **Hand Down Lists**: Parse decisions pages by iterating through dates
2. **Opinion PDFs**: Direct download from opinion URLs
3. **ZIP Files**: Bulk download of daily opinion releases
4. **Justice/Judge Info**: Scrape bio pages for metadata

### Date Handling
- Hand Down dates use `MM-DD-YYYY` format in URLs
- ZIP files use `YYYYMMDD` format
- Calendar interface allows navigation by month/year
- Decisions available back to 1996

### Example API-like Calls
```
# Get Supreme Court decisions for a specific date
GET https://courts.ms.gov/appellatecourts/sc/scdecisions.php?date=01-22-2026

# Download opinion PDF
GET https://courts.ms.gov/appellatecourts/Opinions/CO189869.pdf

# Download ZIP of all SCT opinions for a date
GET https://courts.ms.gov/appellatecourts/ZIPS/SupremeCourt/SC20260122.ZIP
```

## Docket Calendar

The Supreme Court has 6 sittings per year:
- **First Sitting**: January - February
- **Second through Sixth Sittings**: Schedules published as PDF

Calendar PDF URL pattern:
- `https://courts.ms.gov/appellatecourts/sc/docketcalendar/scs{sitting#}{YYYY}.pdf`
- Example: `https://courts.ms.gov/appellatecourts/sc/docketcalendar/scs12026.pdf`

## Notes

1. **Existing Scrapers**: Juriscraper already has Mississippi scrapers (`miss.py`, `missctapp.py`, `miss_beginningofyear.py`, `missctapp_beginningofyear.py`) in the opinions directory.

2. **Hand Down Schedule**: Decisions appear to be released weekly on Wednesdays for the Supreme Court.

3. **Docket Access**: The General Docket requires login, but opinions and hand down lists are publicly accessible.

4. **ZIP Files**: Bulk download of opinions by date is available, making historical scraping efficient.

5. **Oral Arguments**: Live webcasts since October 2014, archived via Vimeo.

6. **JavaScript Requirements**: The decisions calendar uses JavaScript for date selection, but direct URL access with date parameter works.

7. **Trial Court Records**: MEC (Mississippi Electronic Courts) handles e-filing for trial courts (Chancery, Circuit, County) but requires subscription access.
