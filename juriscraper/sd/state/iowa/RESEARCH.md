# Iowa Courts Research

## Court Structure

Iowa has a two-tier appellate court system: the Iowa Supreme Court at the apex and the Iowa Court of Appeals as the intermediate appellate court.

### 1. Iowa Supreme Court
- **Website**: https://www.iowacourts.gov/iowa-courts/supreme-court/
- Highest court in Iowa
- 7 Justices (1 Chief Justice + 6 Associate Justices)
- Justices are appointed by the Governor from nominees submitted by the State Judicial Nominating Commission
- Initial term: one year after appointment until January 1 following the next judicial retention election
- Regular term: 8 years after retention election
- Mandatory retirement age: 72
- Location: Iowa Judicial Branch Building, 1111 East Court Avenue, Des Moines, IA 50319
- Phone: (515) 348-4700 (Clerk of Court)

### 2. Iowa Court of Appeals
- **Website**: https://www.iowacourts.gov/iowa-courts/court-of-appeals/
- Intermediate appellate court
- 9 Judges (1 Chief Judge + 8 Associate Judges)
- Created in 1976
- Judges appointed by the Governor from State Judicial Nominating Commission nominees
- Initial term: one year after appointment until January 1 following the next judicial retention election
- Regular term: 6 years after retention election
- Mandatory retirement age: 72
- Chief Judge elected by court members every two years
- Location: Iowa Judicial Branch Building, 1111 East Court Avenue, Des Moines, IA 50319
- Phone: (515) 348-4700 (Clerk of Court)

## Jurisdiction

### Iowa Supreme Court
- Court of last resort in Iowa
- All opinions are binding on Iowa state courts
- Sole power to admit attorneys to practice in Iowa courts
- Prescribes rules to supervise attorney conduct and discipline
- Promulgates rules of procedure and practice
- Supervisory and administrative control over judicial branch
- Regular sessions held in Des Moines

### Iowa Court of Appeals
- Intermediate appellate court
- Reviews appeals from trial court decisions transferred by the Supreme Court
- Decisions final unless reviewed by Supreme Court on grant of further review
- Some opinions are published and become precedent
- Majority of appeals in Iowa decided by Court of Appeals

## Opinion Types and Publication

### Supreme Court Opinions
- Published opinions with precedential value
- Available from 2017 to present on main site
- Archived opinions prior to November 2017 in separate archive
- PDFs embedded via internal case IDs

### Court of Appeals Opinions
- Published opinions (precedential)
- Unpublished opinions (non-precedential)
- Summaries available for Court of Appeals opinions
- Available from 2018 to present on main site
- Archived opinions from February 7, 2018 and prior in separate archive

### Case Information on Detail Pages
- Case number (appellate)
- Case parties (appellant/appellee or resister/applicant)
- County of origin
- Trial court case number
- Attorneys for parties
- Oral argument schedule
- Briefs (appellant, appellee, reply)
- Embedded YouTube video of oral argument (when available)
- Opinion date and number
- Court of Appeals opinion (if applicable, for further review cases)
- Further review application/resistance documents

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Opinion Listing Structure
- Opinions displayed in paginated lists
- Recent opinions under "Most Recent Opinions" heading
- Past opinions below with pagination
- Pagination: 12 items per page, navigation shows page X of Y
- Archive sidebar with year links

### Case Detail Pages
- Rich information including full case summary
- Embedded PDFs via iframe
- YouTube embedded videos for oral arguments
- Briefs downloadable with size information

### Internal ID System
- Each case has an internal numeric ID (e.g., 22626)
- This ID is used for PDF URLs and brief URLs
- Case number (e.g., 23-1794) used in URL paths
- Mapping between case numbers and internal IDs available via case detail pages

### Case Number Format
- Format: `{YY}-{NNNN}` (e.g., 23-1794, 24-0249)
- Two-digit year prefix
- Four-digit sequential number
- Same format for both Supreme Court and Court of Appeals

### PDF Access
- PDFs embedded in iframes
- Direct access via `/courtcases/{id}/embed/{DocumentType}` URLs
- Document types: SupremeCourtOpinion, CourtAppealsOpinion, FurtherReviewApplication, FurtherReviewResistance
- Briefs via `/courtcases/{id}/briefs/{brief-id}/embedBrief`

## Recommended Scraper Architecture

### 1. Opinion Listing Scraper
**Source**: Opinion archive pages

**Approach**:
1. Start from current year archive page
2. Parse HTML for opinion listings
3. Extract case numbers, titles, dates
4. Navigate pagination if needed
5. Follow links to case detail pages

**Data Available from Listing**:
- Case number
- Case title/parties
- Filing date
- Link to opinion PDF
- Link to summary (Court of Appeals)

### 2. Case Detail Scraper
**Source**: Case detail pages

**Approach**:
1. Navigate to case detail URL
2. Parse HTML for rich case information
3. Extract internal case ID from PDF URLs
4. Download PDFs using embed URLs

**Data Available**:
- Full case style
- County of origin
- Trial court case number
- Attorney names
- Oral argument schedule
- Brief documents
- Oral argument video links
- Opinion summaries

### 3. Oral Arguments Scraper
**Source**: Oral argument schedule pages

**Approach**:
1. Parse schedule tables
2. Extract case numbers, dates, times
3. Follow links to case detail pages
4. Extract YouTube video IDs for archived arguments

**Data Available**:
- Case number
- Case parties
- Argument date/time
- Attorney names
- Video links (YouTube)
