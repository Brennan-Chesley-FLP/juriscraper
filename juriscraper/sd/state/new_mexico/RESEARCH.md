# New Mexico Courts Research

## Court Structure

New Mexico has a two-tier appellate court system with a Supreme Court and an intermediate Court of Appeals.

### 1. New Mexico Supreme Court
- **Website**: https://supremecourt.nmcourts.gov/
- Highest court in New Mexico
- 5 Justices (includes 1 Chief Justice)
- Justices elected in partisan elections for 8-year terms
- Location: 237 Don Gaspar, Santa Fe, NM 87501
- Phone: (505) 827-4860
- Chief Justice: David K. Thomson (appointed April 17, 2024)
- Court established January 6, 1912 (statehood)

#### Jurisdiction
- Original jurisdiction in certain matters (habeas corpus, mandamus, etc.)
- Appellate jurisdiction over Court of Appeals decisions
- Direct appeals in death penalty cases, appeals from Public Regulation Commission, and certain other matters
- Superintending control over all inferior courts and attorneys licensed in the state

### 2. New Mexico Court of Appeals
- **Website**: https://coa.nmcourts.gov/
- Intermediate appellate court created in 1966
- 10 Judges (sit in panels of 3)
- Chief Judge: Jacqueline R. Medina (elected 2018)
- Two locations: Santa Fe and Albuquerque
  - Santa Fe: 237 Don Gaspar, Santa Fe, NM 87501, P.O. Box 2008, Santa Fe, NM 87504-2008
  - Albuquerque: 2211 Tucker NE, Albuquerque, NM 87106

#### Jurisdiction
- Appeals from district courts in civil and criminal matters
- Appeals from administrative agencies
- Most appeals except those reserved for Supreme Court

## Case Number Format

### Supreme Court
- Format: `S-1-SC-{number}`
- Example: `S-1-SC-40434` (Burns v. Presbyterian Healthcare Servs.)
- The `S-1-SC` prefix indicates Supreme Court

### Court of Appeals
- Format: `A-1-CA-{number}`
- Example: `A-1-CA-38594` (Burns v. Presbyterian Healthcare Servs. - Court of Appeals level)
- The `A-1-CA` prefix indicates Court of Appeals

### Official Citations
- **Supreme Court Published**: `{year}-NMSC-{number}`
  - Example: `2017-NMSC-017`
- **Court of Appeals Published**: `{year}-NMCA-{number}`
  - Example: `1999-NMCA-075`
- **Certiorari Tables**: `{year}-NMCERT-{number}`
  - Example: `2025-NMCERT-001`

## Opinion Types and Publication

### NMOneSource Collections

#### Supreme Court of New Mexico
- **Slip Opinions**: First version released by Chief Clerk, may contain deviations from final authenticated opinion
- **Unreported Opinions**: Non-precedential opinions
- Historical coverage from 1852 to present

#### Court of Appeals of New Mexico
- **Slip Opinions**: Published opinions
- **Unreported Opinions**: Non-precedential opinions (formerly "memorandum opinions")
- Historical coverage from 1966 to present (Court created in 1966)

### Additional Collections on NMOneSource
- Current New Mexico Statutes Annotated 1978
- Current New Mexico Rules Annotated
- Attorney General Opinions and Advisory Letters
- State Ethics Commission Advisory Opinions
- New Mexico Administrative Code
- New Mexico Session Laws
- New Mexico Territorial Laws and Treaties (historical)

### Document Formats
- Opinions: HTML (with embedded citations) or PDF download
- Oral argument recordings: MP3 audio
- Case reports: HTML pages or PDF

## Technical Implementation Notes

### NMOneSource Platform
- **Technology**: Powered by Norma (by Lexum) - https://lexum.com/en/norma/
- **Content Loading**: Uses iframe for main content (`decisia-iframe-content`)
- **Search Syntax**: Supports `""`, OR, AND, EXACT(), /p, /s, /n operators
- **Note**: Docket numbers may contain commas (e.g., No. 27,270)

### Supreme Court Website
- **Platform**: WordPress with LiteSpeed caching
- **Assets Location**: `https://supremecourt.nmcourts.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/`

### Data Available Per Opinion (NMOneSource)
- Collection (Court name)
- Date
- Docket Number(s)
- Decision-maker(s) (Justices/Judges)
- Opinion Type (Slip Opinions, Unreported Opinions)
- Related documents (AI summaries)
- Citations to NM Laws and Court Rules
- Citations to NM Appellate Reports
- Full text with hyperlinked citations

### Scraping Approach
1. **Primary Source - NMOneSource**: Parse opinion listings by date for both courts
2. **Oral Arguments**: Parse Supreme Court recordings page for MP3 links
3. **Judge Information**: Scrape bio pages from court websites

### Date Handling
- Opinion dates: MM/DD/YYYY format
- NMOneSource year navigation available

## Notes

1. **NMOneSource is the Official Legal Research Tool**: Maintained by the New Mexico Compilation Commission, this is the authoritative source for opinions, statutes, and court rules.

2. **Historical Coverage**: Supreme Court opinions available from 1852 (territorial period); Court of Appeals from 1966 (when created).

3. **AI-Generated Summaries**: NMOneSource now provides AI-generated opinion summaries as a separate collection.

4. **Vendor-Neutral Citations**: New Mexico adopted vendor-neutral citations per Rule 23-112 NMRA.

5. **Case Lookup Limitations**: The caselookup.nmcourts.gov portal uses CAPTCHA and is not suitable for automated scraping. Use NMOneSource instead for opinion retrieval.

6. **Two Court Locations**: Both appellate courts maintain offices in Santa Fe and Albuquerque.

7. **Court of Appeals Panels**: The 10 judges sit in panels of 3 to decide cases.

8. **Oral Argument Recordings**: Supreme Court recordings available from July 2017 onwards via website; contact Clerk for earlier recordings.

9. **New Mexico Compilation Commission**: https://www.nmcompcomm.us/ - Responsible for official legal publications.

10. **Scope of Coverage**: https://www.nmcompcomm.us/scope-of-coverage-2/ - Details on what is included in NMOneSource.
