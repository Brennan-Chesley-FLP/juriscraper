# Wisconsin Courts Research

## Court Structure

Wisconsin has a unified court system with two appellate courts.

### 1. Supreme Court of Wisconsin
- **Website**: https://www.wicourts.gov/courts/supreme/index.htm
- Highest court in Wisconsin (court of last resort)
- 7 Justices elected to 10-year terms in statewide, non-partisan April elections
- Location: State Capitol, Madison
- Chief Justice: Jill J. Karofsky (as of July 1, 2025)

#### Jurisdiction
- Appellate jurisdiction over all Wisconsin courts
- Discretionary review - selects which cases to hear
- Original actions (cases not previously heard in another Wisconsin court)
- Administrative and regulatory authority over all Wisconsin courts and lawyers

### 2. Court of Appeals of Wisconsin
- **Website**: https://www.wicourts.gov/courts/appeals/index.htm
- Intermediate appellate court
- 16 judges from four geographic districts, elected to 6-year terms
- Chief Judge: Maxine A. White

#### Four Districts:
- **District I** (Milwaukee): Milwaukee County only
  - Location: 330 East Kilbourn Avenue, Suite 1020, Milwaukee
- **District II** (Waukesha): Calumet, Fond du Lac, Green Lake, Kenosha, Manitowoc, Ozaukee, Racine, Sheboygan, Walworth, Washington, Waukesha, Winnebago
  - Location: 2727 N. Grandview Blvd., Suite 300, Waukesha
- **District III** (Wausau): Ashland, Barron, Bayfield, Brown, Buffalo, Burnett, Chippewa, Door, Douglas, Dunn, Eau Claire, Florence, Forest, Iron, Kewaunee, Langlade, Lincoln, Marathon, Marinette, Menominee, Oconto, Oneida, Outagamie, Pepin, Pierce, Polk, Price, Rusk, Sawyer, Shawano, St. Croix, Taylor, Trempealeau, Vilas, Washburn
  - Location: 2100 Stewart Avenue, Suite 310, Wausau
- **District IV** (Madison): Adams, Clark, Columbia, Crawford, Dane, Dodge, Grant, Green, Iowa, Jackson, Jefferson, Juneau, La Crosse, Lafayette, Marquette, Monroe, Portage, Richland, Rock, Sauk, Vernon, Waupaca, Waushara, Wood
  - Location: 2921 Landmark Place, Suite 415, Madison

### 3. Trial Courts
- **Circuit Courts**: General jurisdiction trial courts (72 counties)
- **Municipal Courts**: Limited jurisdiction for ordinance violations

## Opinion Types and Document Categories

### Supreme Court
- **Opinions**: Full written opinions with precedential value
- **Dispositional Orders**: Orders disposing of cases
- **Administrative Orders**: Non-case-specific court orders
- **Rules**: Court rule changes and amendments

### Court of Appeals
- **Published Opinions**: Precedential value
- **Unpublished Opinions**: No precedential value (not to be cited as binding authority)
- **Summary Dispositions**: Summary opinion in order form
- **Certifications**: Requests by Court of Appeals for Supreme Court to take jurisdiction

### Publication Orders
- **Orders of Published Opinions**: Monthly court orders listing published cases
- **Orders of Unpublished Opinions**: Orders listing opinions that will not be published
- **Unpublished Opinion Table**: https://www.wicourts.gov/other/appeals/unpubtable.jsp

## Case Number Formats

### Appeal Numbers
- Format: `YYYYAPNNNNNN` (e.g., `2023AP2319`)
- Shortened input format: `YYapNNNN` (e.g., `23ap2319` for `2023AP002319`)

### Circuit Court Case Numbers
- Format: `YYYYCCNNNNNN` (e.g., `2002CF000001`)
- CC = Case type (CF = Criminal Felony, CV = Civil, etc.)
- Shortened input format: `YYccN` (e.g., `02cf1` for `2002CF000001`)

### Public Domain Citations
- Supreme Court: `YYYY WI NN` (e.g., `2026 WI 3`)
- Court of Appeals: `YYYY WI App NN`

### Official Publisher Citations
- Wisconsin Reports 2d: `NNN Wis. 2d NNN`
- Northwest Reporter 2d: `NNN N.W.2d NNN`

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Website Architecture
- **Primary Domain**: www.wicourts.gov
- **Appellate Case Access**: wscca.wicourts.gov
- **Circuit Court Access**: wcca.wicourts.gov
- **Appellate eFiling**: acefiling.wicourts.gov
- **Circuit eFiling**: efiling.wicourts.gov

### Opinion Pages Structure
- Supreme Court and Court of Appeals have separate opinion pages
- Court of Appeals opinions separated by:
  - Published opinions
  - Unpublished opinions
  - Summary dispositions
  - Certifications

### Opinion Search Form Fields (Supreme Court)
- Case number
- Date range (Today, This week, Last week, This month, Last month, or custom range)
- Party name (last)
- Disposition (dropdown with many options)
- Citation type (NW2D, Wis2D, or None)
- Public domain citation number
- Sort by (Release date or Case number)
- Keyword search (separate form)

### WSCCA Search Fields
- Appeal Number
- Circuit Court Case Number
- Party Name (Last, First, Middle)
- Business Name
- Attorney Name (Last, First)
- County (dropdown, 72 counties + Statewide)
- Court Type (Court of Appeals, Supreme Court)
- District (1, 2, 3, 4)
- Filing Date (with date range options)
- Case Status (Closed, Open/Reopened, Pending Remit, Pending Petition for Review, Remanded)
- Class Code

### JavaScript Requirements
- Main opinion pages work without JavaScript
- WSCCA requires JavaScript for full functionality
- eFiling portals require JavaScript

### PDF Format
- Opinions provided as PDF documents
- Direct download via URL patterns with seqNo or docId parameters

### Existing Scrapers
Check `juriscraper/opinions/united_states/state/` for any existing Wisconsin scrapers.

## Notes

1. **Opinion Release Schedule**: Supreme Court opinions are released by the end of the September-June session. On release day, opinions are available at approximately 8:30 a.m. CDT. Court of Appeals opinions are released daily at approximately 8:30 a.m. CDT.

2. **Precedential Value**: Court of Appeals unpublished opinions have no precedential value and cannot be cited as binding authority.

3. **Chief Justice Selection**: The Wisconsin Supreme Court elects its chief justice from among the seven justices for two-year terms.

4. **Four Districts**: The Court of Appeals has four geographic districts, each with a presiding judge appointed by the chief judge.

5. **Justice on Wheels**: The Supreme Court occasionally holds oral arguments outside Madison as part of its outreach and education program.

6. **WSCCA Maintenance**: The system may be down for maintenance every night from 3:00 AM to 4:00 AM Central Standard Time.

7. **Briefs Availability**: eFiled briefs from July 1, 2009 forward are available on WSCCA. Older briefs are available through the University of Wisconsin Law Library repository.

8. **Case Status Codes**: WSCCA uses specific status codes (CL=Closed, OP=Open, RO=Reopened, PR=Pending Remit, RE=Pending Petition for Review, RM=Remanded).

9. **Recent Leadership Changes**: Justice Ann Walsh Bradley served briefly as Chief Justice (May 1 - June 30, 2025) before Justice Karofsky assumed the role on July 1, 2025.

10. **Two-Factor Authentication**: eCourts applications require two-factor authentication as of September 2025.
