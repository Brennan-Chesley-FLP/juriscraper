# Hawaii Courts Research

## Court Structure

Hawaii has a two-tier appellate court system with the Supreme Court of Hawaii at the apex and the Intermediate Court of Appeals (ICA) as the intermediate appellate court.

### 1. Supreme Court of Hawaii
- **Website**: https://www.courts.state.hi.us/courts/supreme/hawaii_supreme_court
- Highest court in Hawaii
- 5 Justices (1 Chief Justice + 4 Associate Justices)
- Justices serve 10-year terms
- Must be licensed to practice law in Hawaii for at least 10 years before nomination
- Mandatory retirement at age 70
- Location: Aliʻiōlani Hale, Honolulu

### 2. Intermediate Court of Appeals (ICA)
- **Website**: https://www.courts.state.hi.us/courts/appeals/intermediate_court_of_appeals
- Intermediate appellate court
- 7 Judges (currently 6 listed on website)
- Hears nearly all appeals from trial courts and some state agencies
- Judges serve 10-year terms

## Opinion PDF URL Patterns

### Supreme Court and ICA Opinions
- `https://www.courts.state.hi.us/wp-content/uploads/{YYYY}/{MM}/{case_number}{suffix}.pdf`
- Examples:
  - `https://www.courts.state.hi.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CAAP-23-0000347sdo.pdf`
  - `https://www.courts.state.hi.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/SCWC-24-0000450certrej.pdf`
  - `https://www.courts.state.hi.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/SCPW-25-0000710ord.pdf`

### Opinion Suffixes
| Suffix | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| sdo | Summary Disposition Order |
| dso | Dismissal Order (variant) |
| mop | Memorandum Opinion |
| ord | Order |
| certrej | Certiorari Rejected |
| certerej | Certiorari Rejection (variant spelling) |
| recond | Reconsideration |
| dsm | Dismissal |

## Case Number Patterns

### Supreme Court Cases
- **SCWC-**: Supreme Court Writ of Certiorari (from ICA)
  - Format: `SCWC-{YY}-{seq}` (e.g., SCWC-24-0000450)
- **SCPW-**: Supreme Court Public Writer/Petition for Writ
  - Format: `SCPW-{YY}-{seq}` (e.g., SCPW-25-0000710)

### Intermediate Court of Appeals Cases
- **CAAP-**: Court of Appeals - Appeal
  - Format: `CAAP-{YY}-{seq}` (e.g., CAAP-23-0000347)
  - 7-digit sequence number (0000XXX)

## Jurisdiction

### Supreme Court of Hawaii
- Hears appeals via writs of certiorari from the ICA
- Designated direct appeals from agency decisions
- Applications for transfer from the ICA
- Reserved questions of law from circuit courts, land court, and tax appeal court
- Certified questions of law from federal courts
- Applications for writs to judges and other public officers
- Election complaints
- Makes rules of practice and procedure for all state courts
- Licenses, regulates, and disciplines attorneys
- Disciplines judges

### Intermediate Court of Appeals
- Hears nearly all appeals from trial courts
- Appeals from some state agencies
- Discretionary authority for cases submitted without prior suit (agreed facts)
- Cases may be transferred to Supreme Court
- ICA judgments subject to Supreme Court discretionary review via writ of certiorari

## Opinion Types and Publication

### Publication Categories
1. **For Publication**: Designated for publication in West's Hawaii Reports and West's Pacific Reporter, may be cited as precedent under HRAP Rule 35
2. **Not for Publication**: Not published in official reporters, has limited precedential value

### Opinion Types
- Published Opinions
- Memorandum Opinions
- Summary Disposition Orders
- Other final orders disposing of appeals or original proceedings

### Publication Schedule
- Opinions on the merits are posted to the website by 2 p.m. on the same day they are filed (barring unforeseen circumstances)
- Documents are posted "as filed" without enhancement or editing
- Subject to reconsideration under HRAP Rule 40

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Website Platform
- WordPress-based website
- Opinions stored in WordPress uploads directory structure
- RSS feed available for automated polling

### Opinion URL Structure
- All opinions in `/wp-content/uploads/` directory
- Organized by year (YYYY) and month (MM)
- File naming: `{case_number}{suffix}.pdf`

### Case Number Parsing
- Supreme Court: SCWC-YY-XXXXXXX (certiorari), SCPW-YY-XXXXXXX (writ petitions)
- ICA: CAAP-YY-XXXXXXX (appeals)
- 7-digit sequence numbers padded with leading zeros

### Scraping Considerations
- Static HTML pages (no complex JavaScript rendering required)
- WordPress media upload URLs predictable
- RSS feed useful for monitoring new opinions
- No apparent rate limiting, but respectful scraping recommended

## Recommended Scraper Architecture

### 1. Opinion Listing Scraper
**Source**: https://www.courts.state.hi.us/opinions_and_orders/opinions

**Approach**:
1. Parse HTML for opinion entries organized by date
2. Extract case numbers and PDF URLs from WordPress content
3. Follow links to PDF files
4. Use RSS feed for monitoring new opinions

**Data Available**:
- Case number
- Case name/style
- Opinion date
- Publication status (For Publication / Not for Publication)
- Opinion type (SDO, Memorandum Opinion, etc.)
- PDF URL

### 2. RSS Feed Monitor
**Source**: https://www.courts.state.hi.us/opinions-orders/feed

**Approach**:
1. Poll RSS feed periodically for new entries
2. Parse feed for new opinions
3. Extract case details and PDF links
4. Download opinions

### 3. Case Search Integration
**Source**: eCourt Kokua system

**Approach**:
1. Research eCourt Kokua API/interface
2. Query for appellate case information
3. Link to opinion PDFs where available

### Scraping Considerations
- WordPress-based site with predictable URL patterns
- RSS feed available for efficient polling
- PDFs stored in standard WordPress uploads directory
- No JavaScript rendering required for opinion listings
- Respectful request timing recommended
