# Kansas Courts Research

## Court Structure

Kansas has a two-tier appellate court system: the Kansas Supreme Court at the apex and the Kansas Court of Appeals as the intermediate appellate court.

### 1. Kansas Supreme Court
- **Website**: https://kscourts.gov/About-the-Courts/Supreme-Court
- Highest court in Kansas
- 7 Justices (1 Chief Justice + 6 Associate Justices)
- Justices selected through merit-based nomination process added to Kansas Constitution in 1958
- Nine-member Supreme Court Nominating Commission reviews nominees; Governor makes appointments
- After first full year, justice must stand for retention vote in next general election
- If retained, serves 6-year terms with retention votes every 6 years
- Chief Justice: Justice with most years of service (unless declined/resigned)
- Location: Kansas Judicial Center, 301 SW 10th Avenue, Topeka, Kansas 66612-1507

### 2. Kansas Court of Appeals
- **Website**: https://kscourts.gov/About-the-Courts/Court-of-Appeals
- Intermediate appellate court
- 14 Judges (1 Chief Judge + 13 Associate Judges)
- Located in Kansas Judicial Center in Topeka
- Judges appointed by Governor and confirmed by majority vote of Senate
- 4-year terms, retained for successive terms by public vote
- Chief Judge selected by Supreme Court
- Judges sit in three-judge panels at locations throughout state

## Jurisdiction

### Kansas Supreme Court
- Court of last resort in Kansas
- Decisions set binding legal precedent for lower courts
- General administrative supervision over court affairs and judicial branch
- Reviews appeals from Court of Appeals
- Mandatory jurisdiction over certain cases (death penalty, etc.)
- Six one-week sessions in Topeka per year
- Occasional one-day travel dockets at different locations
- Live broadcasts of oral arguments

### Kansas Court of Appeals
- Intermediate appellate court
- Hears appeals from district courts in civil or criminal cases (except those appealed directly to Supreme Court)
- Hears appeals from administrative agencies:
  - Kansas Corporation Commission
  - Kansas Board of Tax Appeals
  - Kansas Workers Compensation Appeals Board
- Judges decide cases by reviewing trial record, written briefs, and hearing oral arguments
- Some decisions published in bound volumes

## Opinion Types and Publication

### Published Decisions
- Printed in bound volumes:
  - Kansas Reports (Supreme Court)
  - Kansas Court of Appeals Reports (Court of Appeals)
- "Slip opinions" are posted online shortly after filing
- Online slip opinions available from October 25, 1996

### Unpublished Decisions
- Do not decide new points of law
- Will not be published in bound volumes
- Available online same day as filing
- Online unpublished decisions from September 4, 2015

### Release Schedule
- Decisions usually released around 9:30 a.m. on Fridays
- Can be filed any day of the week
- Posted online as soon as filed

### Decision Information Available
- Case number (6-digit format, e.g., 126317)
- Case title/parties
- Court (Supreme Court or Court of Appeals)
- Release date
- Publication status (Published/Unpublished)
- Direct PDF link

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Decision Search System
- **Domain**: searchdro.kscourts.gov
- **Framework**: Appears to be ASP.NET-based
- **Search interface**: DataTables JavaScript library for table display
- **Pagination**: Client-side with server data
- **Sorting**: Columns sortable (Release Date, Case Number, Case Title, Court, Status)
- **Filter**: Client-side text filter on displayed results

### Decision Listing by Date
- Organized by year, then by date
- Separate sections for Supreme Court and Court of Appeals
- Lists case number, title, and publication status

### Case Number Format
- **Format**: 6-digit number (e.g., 126317, 128762)
- No hyphens or year prefixes in Kansas case numbers
- Numbers appear to be sequential across both courts

### PDF Structure
- PDFs hosted at searchdro.kscourts.gov
- Each PDF has unique UUID plus case number in filename
- Direct download links available from search results
- Example: `/documents/pdf/caseDecisions/{uuid}_{case-number}.pdf`

### Main Website (kscourts.gov)
- Kentico CMS
- Responsive design
- Some CSP restrictions visible in console

## Recommended Scraper Architecture

### 1. Decision Search Scraper
**Source**: https://searchdro.kscourts.gov/Documents/LoadPage

**Approach**:
1. Access the decision search page
2. Set date range parameters (or use default 3-month window for recent)
3. Select court filter (Supreme Court, Court of Appeals, or Both)
4. Select publication status filter (Published, Unpublished, or Both)
5. Parse results table for case information
6. Extract PDF links directly from table
7. Handle pagination (10/25/50/100 entries per page)

**Data Available from Search**:
- Release date
- Case number
- Case title
- Court
- Publication status
- Direct PDF URL

### 2. Decision by Date Scraper
**Source**: https://searchdro.kscourts.gov/Data/decisionsList

**Approach**:
1. Navigate to decisions list page
2. Parse hierarchical structure (Year > Date > Cases)
3. Extract case numbers and titles
4. Separate Supreme Court from Court of Appeals
5. Follow PDF links

**Data Available**:
- Date organization
- Case numbers
- Case titles
- Publication status (Published/Unpublished)
- PDF links

### 3. Oral Arguments Scraper
**Source**: Oral argument archive pages

**Approach**:
1. Navigate to year-specific archive pages
2. Parse case listings with oral argument dates
3. Extract YouTube video links
4. Cross-reference with case details

**Data Available**:
- Case numbers
- Argument dates
- YouTube video links
- Case parties
