# Minnesota Courts Research

## Court Structure

Minnesota has a unified court system with the following appellate courts:

### 1. Minnesota Supreme Court
- **Website**: https://mncourts.gov/supremecourt
- Highest court in Minnesota
- 7 Justices (1 Chief Justice + 6 Associate Justices)
- Justices are elected on a non-partisan, statewide ballot to 6-year terms
- Handles discretionary review of Court of Appeals decisions
- Direct appellate jurisdiction over first-degree murder cases, Tax Court, and Workers' Compensation Court of Appeals
- Published opinions serve as binding precedent
- Reviews 600+ petitions per year, accepting about 10-14% for review
- Court term runs September through June
- Opinions typically released on Wednesdays

### 2. Minnesota Court of Appeals
- **Website**: https://mncourts.gov/courtofappeals
- Intermediate appellate court (established November 1, 1983)
- 19 Judges who sit in three-judge panels
- Chief Judge: Jennifer L. Frisch
- Handles most appeals from trial courts, state agencies, and local governments
- "Error-correcting court" - decisions are final in about 95% of appeals
- Handles 2,000-2,400 appeals per year
- Must issue decisions within 90 days of oral arguments (shortest deadline in nation)
- Issues both precedential (published) and nonprecedential (unpublished) opinions
- Opinions released weekly on Mondays at 10:00 a.m. CST/CDT (Tuesday if Monday is a holiday)
- Judges travel throughout Minnesota to hear oral arguments

## Opinion Types and Publication

### Supreme Court Opinions
- **Citation Format**: `___ N.W.2d ___` (official reporter)
- Binding precedent on all Minnesota courts
- Released on Wednesdays (typically)
- Media release time is 10:00 A.M.

### Court of Appeals - Precedential Opinions
- **Citation Format**: `___ N.W.2d ___` (official reporter)
- Formerly called "published opinions"
- Binding precedent under Minn. Rule of Civil Appellate Procedure 136.01
- Issued only to clarify or develop the law
- Factors considered: establishes new principle, clarifies existing case law, decides novel constitutional/statutory issue, resolves significant recurring legal issue

### Court of Appeals - Nonprecedential Opinions
- Not binding precedent
- May be cited for persuasive value
- Available weekly alongside precedential opinions

### Docket Number Format
- Case numbers include hyphen after year: `A25-0064`
- Format: `A{YY}-{NNNN}` (A = Appeal, YY = year, NNNN = sequence)
- Supreme Court and Court of Appeals use same format
- In P-MACS searches, include the hyphen

## Access Restrictions

### No Login Required
- All court websites are publicly accessible
- Published opinions freely downloadable as PDFs
- P-MACS case search available without authentication
- ZIP files of opinions available for download

### P-MACS (Public Minnesota Appellate Courts System)
- **URL**: https://macsnc.courts.state.mn.us/ctrack/publicLogin.jsp
- Requires accepting terms of use
- Provides access to cases open on or after March 3, 2003
- Data available: case title, appellate case number, parties, attorneys, docket entries, links to orders/opinions
- Older archived cases (before March 3, 2003): only case title, case number, and abbreviated party info
- Some documents marked "sealed" refer to remote access restrictions only
- Full access may be available at courthouse public terminals
- Note: "Exclude Closed/Archived" checkbox is checked by default - uncheck to search closed cases
- Support email: pmacs.support@courts.state.mn.us
- Phone: (651) 291-5297

### Clerk of Appellate Courts
- Located at: 305 Minnesota Judicial Center, 25 Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd., St. Paul
- Phone: (651) 291-5297
- Hours: 8:00 AM - 4:30 PM, Monday-Friday

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Website Structure
- **Domain**: mncourts.gov
- Modern responsive website
- Judges/Justices pages load dynamically via JavaScript API
- API endpoint pattern: `?collection=mjb~sp-search&query=&profile=judicial-officers&...`

### P-MACS Case Search Interface
- **URL**: https://macsnc.courts.state.mn.us/ctrack/search/publicCaseSearch.do
- Search fields:
  - Case Number
  - Case Title
  - Case Group (Abbreviated Adversarial, Administrative Files, Other, Professional Regulation, Standard)
  - Jurisdiction (Court of Appeals, Supreme Court)
  - Status (Archived, Briefing, Closed, Court Hearing, Decision, Open, Panel Hearing, Petition, PFR, Post-Decision, Pre-Appeal, Pre-Briefing, Pre-Hearing, Referee, Stayed)
  - Case Type (Appeal Panel, Attorney, Board-Commission, Civil, Criminal, Election Contest, Judge, Writs, etc.)
  - Date Range
  - Case SubType (extensive list including 1st Deg. Murder, Tax Court, Workers' Comp, etc.)
- Participant Search also available

### Opinion Listing Structure
On opinions pages, listings include:
- Case number with link to PDF
- Case name/parties
- Lower court information
- Summary/holding
- Author information

### Data Available for Each Opinion
- Appellate case number
- Case name/parties
- Decision date
- PDF document
- Author (judge/justice)
- Outcome (Affirmed, Reversed, etc.)
- Lower court and judge

## Recommended Scraper Architecture

### 1. Minnesota Supreme Court Opinion Scraper
**Source**: https://mncourts.gov/supremecourt/recentopinions/minnesota-supreme-court-opinion

**Approach**:
1. Navigate to opinions page
2. Parse opinion listings for case numbers, names, dates
3. Extract direct PDF URLs from links
4. Handle weekly releases (typically Wednesdays)

**Data Available**:
- Case number
- Case name
- Decision date
- Direct PDF URL
- Author
- Outcome

### 2. Minnesota Court of Appeals Opinion Scraper
**Source**: https://mncourts.gov/courtofappeals/recentopinions/precedential-opinions and /nonprecedential-opinions

**Approach**:
1. Access both precedential and nonprecedential pages
2. Parse weekly opinion listings
3. Extract PDF URLs and metadata
4. Handle Monday releases (Tuesday if holiday)

**Data Available**:
- Case number
- Case name
- Decision date
- Lower court and judge
- Author
- Outcome
- Precedential vs. nonprecedential status

### 3. P-MACS Case Search Scraper
**Source**: https://macsnc.courts.state.mn.us/ctrack/search/publicCaseSearch.do

**Approach**:
1. Accept terms of use
2. Submit search queries with filters
3. Parse search results for case metadata
4. Navigate to individual case pages for docket entries and documents

**Considerations**:
- Must accept terms on each session
- Case number format requires hyphen (A25-0064, not A250064)
- "Exclude Closed/Archived" checked by default

### 4. Oral Arguments Scraper
**Source**: https://mncourts.gov/supremecourt/calendar

**Approach**:
1. Parse monthly calendar table for PDF links
2. Download and parse calendar PDFs for argument schedules
3. Extract case information from issue summaries

## Notes

1. **Opinion Release Schedule**: Supreme Court releases opinions on Wednesdays at 10:00 AM. Court of Appeals releases opinions on Mondays at 10:00 AM CST/CDT (Tuesday if Monday is a holiday).

2. **Precedential vs. Nonprecedential**: Minnesota uses "precedential" rather than "published" terminology for binding Court of Appeals opinions as of recent rule changes.

3. **P-MACS Limitations**: Not all documents are available for remote viewing. Some marked as "sealed" may still be accessible at courthouse terminals.

4. **E-Filing**: Electronic filing and notification is mandatory. Attorneys and parties receive notices, orders, opinions, and correspondence via email.

5. **90-Day Decision Requirement**: Court of Appeals must issue decisions within 90 days of oral arguments or conference date - the shortest deadline of any appellate court in the nation.

6. **Historical Data**: P-MACS provides full case information for cases open on or after March 3, 2003. Older cases have limited information available.

7. **Opinion Archive**: The Minnesota State Law Library maintains a comprehensive archive of appellate opinions searchable at http://mn.gov/law-library/search/index.jsp?v:sources=mn-law-library-opinions
