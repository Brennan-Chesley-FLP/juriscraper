# Virginia Courts Research

## Court Structure

Virginia has a unified court system with two appellate courts at the apex.

### 1. Supreme Court of Virginia
- **Website**: https://www.vacourts.gov/courts/scv/home
- Highest court in Virginia (court of last resort)
- 7 Justices (1 Chief Justice, 6 Associate Justices) serving 12-year terms
- 2 Senior Justices (retired justices who may be recalled)
- Location: 100 North Ninth Street, 5th Floor, Richmond, VA 23219-1315
- Phone: (804) 786-2251
- Filing Fee: $50.00
- Hours: Monday - Friday, 8:15 AM - 4:45 PM

#### Jurisdiction
- **Appellate Jurisdiction**: Primary function is to review decisions of lower tribunals
- **No Right of Appeal**: Virginia does not allow appeals to the Supreme Court as a matter of right except for:
  - Cases involving the State Corporation Commission
  - Certain disciplinary actions against attorneys
- **Original Jurisdiction** (limited):
  - Habeas corpus
  - Mandamus
  - Prohibition
  - Actual innocence (based on biological testing)
  - Judicial Inquiry and Review Commission matters relating to judicial censure, retirement, and removal

#### Clerk of Court
- Muriel-Theresa Pitney (Clerk of Court)
- William "Basil" Tsimpris (Chief Deputy Clerk)
- Jody James-Woods (Senior Deputy Clerk)

### 2. Court of Appeals of Virginia
- **Website**: https://www.vacourts.gov/courts/cav/home
- Intermediate appellate court established January 1, 1985
- Created to increase appellate capacity and expedite appellate review
- 17 Judges
- Administrative headquarters: Supreme Court Building, Richmond, Virginia
- Location: 109 North Eighth Street, Richmond, VA 23219-2321
- Phone: (804) 786-5651
- Filing Fee: $50.00
- Hours: Monday - Friday, 8:15 AM - 4:45 PM

#### Clerk of Court
- A. John Vollino

### 3. Trial Courts
- **Circuit Courts**: General jurisdiction (31 judicial circuits)
- **General District Courts**: Limited jurisdiction (32 districts)
- **Juvenile and Domestic Relations District Courts**: Juvenile matters (32 districts)
- **Magistrate Services**: Preliminary hearings, warrants (32 districts)

## Opinion Types and Document Categories

### Supreme Court
- **Published Opinions**: Full written opinions with precedential value, released typically on Thursdays
- **Unpublished Orders**: Orders for cases argued before the full court
- **Published Orders**: Administrative and procedural orders

### Court of Appeals
- **Published Opinions**: Opinions designated by the Court as having precedential value or significance for the law or legal system. Released every Tuesday.
- **Unpublished Opinions**: Opinions NOT designated as having precedential value. Released every Tuesday.

## Case Number Formats

### Supreme Court
- Format: `YYNNNN` (6 digits)
- Example: `240736` (Year 2024, case 736)
- PDF naming: `1{case_number}.pdf` (prefix with 1)
  - Example: File `1240736.pdf` for case `240736`

### Court of Appeals
- Format: `NNNNNNN` (7 digits)
- Example: `0350251`
- PDF naming: `{case_number}.pdf`

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Website Architecture
- **Primary Domain**: www.vacourts.gov
- **Dynamic Content**: webdev.vacourts.gov/dynamic/ (opinion pages)
- **Case Management**: eapps.courts.state.va.us (ACMS systems)
- **Static Files**: www.vacourts.gov/static/ and www.vacourts.gov/opinions/

### Opinion Pages Structure
- Opinions listed chronologically with most recent first
- Each opinion entry contains:
  - Case number (linked to PDF)
  - Case name
  - Date
  - Summary/disposition

### HTML Structure (Opinions Pages)
- Opinions are in `<p>` elements
- Links (`<a>`) contain case number and PDF URL
- Case name in `<b>` tags
- Date and summary as text content

### Existing Scrapers
Virginia already has working scrapers in juriscraper:
- `va.py` - Supreme Court of Virginia opinions
- `vactapp_p.py` - Court of Appeals published opinions
- `vactapp_u.py` - Court of Appeals unpublished opinions

### JavaScript Requirements
- Main opinion pages work without JavaScript
- Some interactive elements (accordions on court pages) require JavaScript
- ACMS case search systems require JavaScript and cookies

### PDF Format
- Opinions provided as Adobe Acrobat PDF documents
- Direct download via URL pattern

## Notes

1. **Discretionary Review**: Virginia does not allow appeals to the Supreme Court as a matter of right in most cases. The Court exercises discretionary review through petitions for appeal.

2. **Opinion Release Schedule**:
   - Supreme Court: Typically released on Thursdays
   - Court of Appeals: Released every Tuesday

3. **Court of Appeals Expansion**: The Court of Appeals was established in 1985 as an intermediate appellate court to increase the appellate capacity of the judicial system.

4. **Chief Justice Election**: The Honorable Cleo E. Powell was elected Chief Justice effective January 1, 2026, succeeding Chief Justice S. Bernard Goodwyn who announced retirement.

5. **Electronic Filing**: Available for both appellate courts.

6. **Judicial Circuits**: Virginia has 31 judicial circuits for Circuit Courts.

7. **Live Streaming**: Both courts offer live streaming of oral arguments.

8. **Adobe Acrobat Required**: All opinions are provided as PDF documents.

9. **Legacy URL Pattern**: Some old URLs use `courts.state.va.us` domain which redirects to `vacourts.gov`.

10. **Senior Justices**: Virginia has a system where retired justices may be recalled as Senior Justices.
