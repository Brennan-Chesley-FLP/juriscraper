# Massachusetts Courts Research

## Court Structure

Massachusetts has a well-developed court system with a Supreme Judicial Court (SJC) at the apex and an intermediate Appeals Court. The state's court system is one of the oldest in the nation, with the SJC established in 1692.

### 1. Supreme Judicial Court (SJC)
- **Website**: https://www.mass.gov/orgs/massachusetts-supreme-judicial-court
- Highest court in Massachusetts and oldest appellate court in continuous existence in the Western Hemisphere
- 7 Justices (1 Chief Justice + 6 Associate Justices)
- Justices nominated by the Governor and confirmed by the Governor's Council
- Hears appeals on broad range of criminal and civil cases from September to May
- Also supervises the judiciary and the bar, makes or approves rules for court operations
- Gives advisory opinions upon request to the Governor and Legislature
- **Location**: John Adams Courthouse, 1 Pemberton Square, Suite 2500, Boston, MA 02108
- **Main Phone**: (617) 557-1000

### 2. Appeals Court
- **Website**: https://www.mass.gov/orgs/appeals-court
- Intermediate appellate court established in 1972
- 25 Justices (1 Chief Justice + 24 Associate Justices)
- Court of general appellate jurisdiction reviewing trial court decisions
- Also reviews appeals from Appellate Tax Board, Industrial Accident Board, and Commonwealth Employment Relations Board
- **Location**: John Adams Courthouse, One Pemberton Square, Room 1200, Boston, MA 02108
- **Clerk's Office Phone**: (617) 921-4443

### 3. Trial Court (Seven Departments)
- **Superior Court**: General jurisdiction trial court for serious matters
- **District Court**: Limited jurisdiction court
- **Boston Municipal Court**: City court for Boston
- **Housing Court**: Specialized housing matters
- **Juvenile Court**: Matters involving juveniles
- **Probate and Family Court**: Probate, family law matters
- **Land Court**: Real property disputes

## Key URLs and URL Patterns

### Opinion Sources

#### 1. New Opinions Page (Daily Updates)
- **URL**: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/new-opinions
- Lists today's published opinions and recent decisions
- Published opinions posted at 10 a.m. ET daily
- Notifications available via @MassReports on Twitter at 8 a.m. ET
- Links to individual opinion PDFs on mass.gov

#### 2. LexisNexis Massachusetts Reports Public Access (Primary Archive)
- **URL**: https://www.lexisnexis.com/clients/macourts/
- **Redirects to**: https://advance.lexis.com/container?config=...
- Free public access to official opinions
- **Coverage**:
  - SJC and Appeals Court published opinions from 2001 to present
  - Appeals Court summary dispositions (Rule 23.0/former Rule 1:28) from 2008 to present
- Updated weekly after issuance of advance sheets
- Opinions available within 9 days of release
- Search by court: MA Supreme Judicial Court, MA Appeals Court, MA Appeals Court Unpublished
- Terms of use agreement required before searching
- **Note**: For personal, non-commercial use only

#### 3. 128archive.com (Appeals Court Summary Dispositions)
- **URL**: https://128archive.com/
- Appeals Court summary dispositions pursuant to Rule 23.0 (formerly Rule 1:28)
- **Coverage**: February 25, 2008 to present
- Search by: Docket number, party name, release date range, search terms
- Download PDF or read in full (HTML)
- **Docket Format**: `YY-P-NNNN` (e.g., 24-P-0951)
- Summary dispositions may be cited for persuasive value but not as binding precedent

#### 4. Appellate Opinion Portal
- **URL**: https://www.mass.gov/appellate-opinion-portal
- Central hub linking to all opinion resources
- Links to newest opinions, unofficial collections, summary dispositions

#### 5. Unofficial Collections
- **URL**: https://www.mass.gov/lists/unofficial-collections-of-published-opinions
- Free collections:
  - LexisNexis Massachusetts Reports Public Access
  - Social Law Library (slip opinions only): https://www.socialaw.com/services/slip-opinions
  - Caselaw Access Project: https://case.law
  - CourtListener by Free.Law: https://courtlistener.com
  - Google Scholar
  - FindLaw (SJC and Appeals Court separately)

### Opinion PDF URL Patterns

#### Mass.gov Document Downloads
- **Pattern**: `https://www.mass.gov/doc/{case-name-slug}/download`
- **Examples**:
  - `https://www.mass.gov/doc/commonwealth-v-ortiz-ac-m24p1364/download`
  - `https://www.mass.gov/doc/commonwealth-v-lewis-sjc-m13767/download`
- Slug includes court identifier: `sjc` for Supreme Judicial Court, `ac` for Appeals Court
- Includes modified docket number in slug

#### 128archive.com PDF Downloads
- **Pattern**: `https://128archive.com/Disposition/DownloadDisposition/{id}?dispositionId={dispositionId}`
- **HTML View**: `https://128archive.com/Disposition/ViewDispositionDocInHtml?dispositionId={id}&ispublicview=True`

## Opinion Types and Publication

### Published Opinions (SJC and Appeals Court)
- **Citation Format**: `{Volume} Mass. {Page}` for SJC or `{Volume} Mass. App. Ct. {Page}` for Appeals Court
- Also cited in North Eastern Reporter (N.E.2d, N.E.3d)
- Subject to formal revision; superseded by advance sheets and bound volumes
- Available on LexisNexis archive within 9 days of release

### Appeals Court Summary Dispositions (Rule 23.0)
- **Formerly**: Rule 1:28 (prior to designation change)
- Primarily addressed to parties; may not fully address facts or decisional rationale
- Not circulated to entire court; only represents views of deciding panel
- May be cited for persuasive value since February 25, 2008
- **NOT** binding precedent due to limitations
- Available on 128archive.com

### Docket Number Formats

#### Supreme Judicial Court
- **Format**: `SJC-{NNNNN}` (e.g., SJC-13767)
- Five-digit sequential number

#### Appeals Court
- **Format**: `{YY}-P-{NNNN}` (e.g., 24-P-1364)
- Two-digit year + P + four-digit number

- **Primary Domain**: mass.gov

- PDF and HTML viewing options

- Decision date
- PDF link

- Case name

- PDF download link
- HTML full text view

## Recommended Scraper Architecture

### 1. SJC Published Opinions Scraper
**Primary Source**: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/new-opinions

**Approach**:
1. Parse the new opinions page for SJC decisions
2. Extract case links from list items containing "SJC" in docket number
3. Download PDFs directly from mass.gov/doc/ URLs
4. For historical opinions, use LexisNexis archive API or parse search results

**Data Available**:
- Case name
- Docket number (SJC-NNNNN)
- Decision date
- PDF URL

### 2. Appeals Court Published Opinions Scraper
**Primary Source**: https://www.mass.gov/info-details/new-opinions

**Approach**:
1. Parse the new opinions page for Appeals Court decisions
2. Extract case links from list items containing "AC" in docket number
3. Download PDFs directly from mass.gov/doc/ URLs
4. For historical opinions, use LexisNexis archive

**Data Available**:
- Case name
- Docket number (YY-P-NNNN)
- Decision date
- PDF URL

### 3. Appeals Court Summary Dispositions Scraper
**Primary Source**: https://128archive.com/

**Approach**:
1. Use search interface with date range parameters
2. Parse paginated results
3. Extract docket number, case name, release date
4. Download PDFs using disposition ID URLs

**URL Parameters**:
- `ReleaseDateFrom=MM/DD/YYYY`
- `ReleaseDateTo=MM/DD/YYYY`
- Page number and page size configurable

**Data Available**:
- Docket number
- Case name
- Release date
- PDF download URL
- HTML full text URL

### 4. Oral Arguments Scraper
**SJC Source**: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCOftbmknBche29CG41v19cA
**Appeals Court Source**: https://www.youtube.com/@massappealsct/featured

**Approach**:
1. Use YouTube Data API to list videos
2. Parse video titles for case information
3. Extract argument dates from video metadata

## Notes

1. **Official Reporter**: LexisNexis publishes the official Massachusetts Reports under contract with the state. The LexisNexis public access site is the closest to an "official" free online source.

2. **No Official State Archive**: Unlike some states, Massachusetts does not maintain its own comprehensive online opinion archive. The state relies on the LexisNexis partnership for the official archive.

3. **Two-Court Appellate System**: Massachusetts has a traditional two-tier appellate structure with the SJC as the court of last resort and the Appeals Court handling the bulk of appellate work.

4. **Summary Dispositions**: The 128archive.com site is specifically maintained for Rule 23.0 (formerly 1:28) summary dispositions, which are distinct from published opinions.

5. **Historical Records**: SJC is the oldest appellate court in continuous existence in the Western Hemisphere (established 1692). Historical opinions may be available through specialized archives.

6. **Advance Sheets**: Published opinions appear on the LexisNexis archive after advance sheet publication, within 9 days of initial release. The mass.gov new opinions page provides same-day access to slip opinions.

7. **Citation Practice**: Massachusetts uses parallel citations to both Massachusetts Reports and North Eastern Reporter.

8. **Informal Brief Pilot**: Appeals Court has an ongoing pilot program allowing self-represented parties to file informal briefs (through June 2026).
