# Maine Courts Research

## Court Structure

Maine has a unified court system with the Supreme Judicial Court (also known as the Law Court when sitting in its appellate capacity) at the apex. Unlike many states, Maine does not have an intermediate appellate court.

### 1. Supreme Judicial Court (Law Court)
- **Website**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/sjc/index.html
- Highest court in Maine and court of last resort
- 7 Justices (1 Chief Justice + 6 Associate Justices)
- Justices appointed by the Governor with confirmation by the State Senate
- **Two Major Roles**:
  1. Decides appeals on questions of law from lower courts (sitting as "Law Court")
  2. Governs the Judicial Branch (policy/procedural rules, bar admission, attorney/judge conduct)
- Issues advisory opinions to the Governor or Legislature on "solemn occasions"
- Published opinions are binding on all Maine courts

### 2. Superior Court
- **Website**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/superior/index.html
- Principal trial court for serious matters
- Jurisdiction over almost all civil and criminal matters
- Jury trials available

### 3. District Court
- **Website**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/district/index.html
- Trial court for lesser matters
- Handles lesser criminal offenses, civil actions, and family law matters

### 4. Specialized Courts/Divisions
- **Family Division**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/family/index.html
- **Juvenile Court**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/juvenile/index.html
- **Business and Consumer Court (BCD)**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/bcd/index.html
- **Treatment and Recovery Courts**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/treatment/index.html
- **Violations Bureau (Traffic)**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/traffic/index.html

## Opinion Types and Publication

### Published Opinions
- **Format**: `{Year} ME {Number}` (e.g., "2026 ME 4")
- Full opinions with binding precedential value
- Published online in PDF format since January 2006
- Published online (in some format) since January 1997
- **Official Reporter**: Atlantic Reporter (2d Series and 3d Series) since 1966

### Memoranda of Decision (Unpublished)
- Unpublished decisions listed on the memdec page for 60-90 days after issuance
- **Format**: `Mem {yy}-{nnn}` (e.g., "Mem 26-18")
- Contains: Docket #, Mem Dec #, Case Title, Date, Subject, Trial Judge, Result
- Not binding precedent but available for reference

### Citation Format
- **Standard Citation**: `Dutil v. Burns, 1997 ME 1, ¶ 2, 687 A.2d 639`
- Format: `{Case Name}, {Year} ME {Opinion#}, ¶ {Paragraph}, {Atlantic Reporter Citation}`
- Reference: [Maine Law Review: Uniform Maine Citations](https://mainelaw.maine.edu/academics/journals/uniform-maine-citations/)

### Docket Number Format
- **Pattern**: `{County Abbreviation}-{Year}-{Number}`
- **Examples**:
  - `Kno-24-510` (Knox County, 2024)
  - `BCD-25-229` (Business & Consumer Court, 2025)
  - `Cum-25-196` (Cumberland County, 2025)
  - `Yor-25-269` (York County, 2025)
  - `PUC-25-51` (Public Utilities Commission)
  - `WCB-24-339` (Workers' Compensation Board)

## Access Restrictions

### No Login Required
- All court websites are publicly accessible
- Published opinions freely downloadable as PDFs
- Memoranda of Decision viewable (but not linked as PDFs)
- Justice biographies and court information
- Oral argument recordings on YouTube

### eFiling
- **Maine eCourts**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/ecourts/index.html
- eFiling being rolled out regionally (Region 1 launching January 2026)
- York County scheduled for March 2026

### Notes
- No reCAPTCHA or anti-bot measures observed on opinion pages
- PDFs are direct links without authentication
- Opinions subject to motions for reconsideration per Rule 14(b) of Maine Rules of Appellate Procedure

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Website Structure
- **Domain**: courts.maine.gov
- **Platform**: State of Maine government web template (maine.gov)
- Standard HTML tables for opinion listings
- PDF opinions organized by year in `/courts/sjc/lawcourt/{year}/` directory
- Clean, predictable URL patterns

### Opinion PDF Files
- Naming convention: `{yy}me{nnn}.pdf`
- Two-digit year + "me" + three-digit zero-padded opinion number
- Direct downloadable links
- Standard PDF format

### Data Available for Each Published Opinion
- Opinion number (e.g., "2026 ME 4")
- Case title/parties
- Decision date
- PDF link

### Data Available for Each Memorandum of Decision
- Docket number (county-year-number format)
- Mem Dec number
- Case title
- Date
- Subject matter
- Trial judge
- Result (Affirmed, Dismissed, Vacated, etc.)

### Oral Arguments
- Available on YouTube since January 2024
- Usually grouped by half-day sessions (up to 3 appeals per recording)
- Unedited livestream recordings
- Listed by date and location on recordings page

## Recommended Scraper Architecture

### 1. Maine Supreme Court Opinion Scraper
**Source**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/sjc/opinions.html (current year)
**Archive**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/sjc/lawcourt/{year}/index.html

**Approach**:
1. Navigate to opinions page for target year
2. Parse HTML table containing opinions
3. Extract:
   - Opinion number from first column
   - Case title and PDF URL from second column (link element)
   - Decision date from third column
4. Download PDFs directly using predictable URL pattern

**Data Available**:
- Opinion number (YYYY ME N)
- Case title/parties
- Decision date
- Direct PDF URL

### 2. Memoranda of Decision Scraper
**Source**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/sjc/memdec.html

**Approach**:
1. Parse HTML table on memoranda page
2. Extract all columns:
   - Docket number
   - Mem Dec number
   - Case title
   - Date
   - Subject
   - Trial judge
   - Result
3. Note: PDFs may not be directly linked; may need alternate approach

**Data Available**:
- Full case metadata
- No direct PDF links observed (unpublished decisions)

### 3. Oral Arguments Scraper
**Source**: https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/sjc/recordings.html

**Approach**:
1. Parse the recordings page HTML
2. Extract YouTube links and associated case information
3. Could potentially use YouTube API for additional metadata

**Data Available**:
- Date and location of argument
- Case docket numbers
- YouTube video URLs

## Notes

1. **No Intermediate Appellate Court**: Maine is one of the few states without an intermediate court of appeals. All appeals go directly from trial courts to the Supreme Judicial Court.

2. **Law Court Terminology**: When the Supreme Judicial Court sits in its appellate capacity (deciding appeals), it is referred to as the "Law Court."

3. **Official Reporter**: The Atlantic Reporter has been Maine's official reporter since 1966. Online opinions may have different pagination than the official print version.

4. **Opinion Availability**: Opinions published online since January 2006 are in PDF format. Earlier opinions (back to 1997) are available online in some format.

5. **High School Sessions**: The SJC conducts special sessions at high schools for educational purposes (https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/sjc/arguments-hs.html).

6. **Maine eCourts Rollout**: Electronic court system being deployed regionally starting in 2026.

7. **State of the Judiciary Address**: Annual address by the Chief Justice, available at https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/sjc/soj/soj-2025.pdf
