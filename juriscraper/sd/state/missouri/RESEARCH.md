# Missouri Courts Research

## Court Structure

Missouri has a three-tier court system with one Supreme Court and an intermediate appellate court (Court of Appeals) divided into three regional districts:

### 1. Supreme Court of Missouri
- **Website**: https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=27
- Highest court in Missouri (court of last resort)
- 7 Judges (includes 1 Chief Justice)
- Judges selected under Missouri Nonpartisan Court Plan
- 12-year terms, subject to retention votes
- Mandatory retirement at age 70
- Chief Justice elected by the seven judges to a two-year term (rotates)
- Located at 207 West High Street, Jefferson City, MO 65101
- Phone: (573) 751-4144

#### Exclusive Jurisdiction
- Validity of a United States statute or treaty
- Validity of a Missouri statute or constitutional provision
- State revenue laws
- Challenges to statewide elected official's right to hold office
- Death penalty cases
- Legislative redistricting plans

### 2. Missouri Court of Appeals
- **Website**: https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=261
- Intermediate appellate court
- Divided into three geographic districts
- Handles appeals from circuit courts
- Judges selected under Missouri Nonpartisan Court Plan
- 12-year terms after retention vote

#### Eastern District
- **Website**: https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=99
- Located in St. Louis

#### Southern District
- **Website**: https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=208
- Main offices in Springfield (The Hammons Building)
- Secondary courtroom in Poplar Bluff (Butler County courthouse)
- Covers 44 counties in southern Missouri
- Mandated to hold arguments in both Springfield and Poplar Bluff

#### Western District
- **Website**: https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=227
- Located in Kansas City

### Trial Courts (for reference)
- **Circuit Courts**: 115 counties organized into 46 judicial circuits
- Trial courts of general jurisdiction
- Include divisions: associate circuit, civil/criminal, family, juvenile, municipal, probate

## Case Number Format

Missouri appellate case numbers follow the pattern: `{COURT_PREFIX}{CASE_NUMBER}`

### Court Prefixes
- **SC**: Supreme Court
- **ED**: Eastern District Court of Appeals
- **SD**: Southern District Court of Appeals
- **WD**: Western District Court of Appeals

### Examples
- `SC101157` - Supreme Court case
- `SC100933` - Supreme Court case
- `SD39073` - Southern District Court of Appeals case

## Opinion Types and Publication

### Opinion Listing Information
Each opinion entry on the date page includes:
- Case number (linked to PDF)
- Case name and parties
- Author (Judge name)
- Vote breakdown (e.g., "All concur", "Concurs in part")
- Overview/Summary link (separate PDF)

### Orders
- "Orders Pursuant to Rules 30.25(b) and/or 84.16(b)" - Summary orders
- Listed separately from full opinions

### Historical Coverage
- Opinions available from 1997 to present via the web interface

## Access Restrictions

### No Login Required
- All opinion pages and PDFs are publicly accessible
- Dockets and oral argument schedules publicly available
- Judge bio pages publicly accessible
- Case.net basic case information publicly accessible

### Login/Restrictions
- **Remote Public Access to Documents**: Documents filed before July 1, 2023 only viewable at courthouse terminals
- **Case.net Document Access**: Remote access to documents filed after July 1, 2023 may require redaction compliance
- **Case.net Hours**: Available Monday-Friday, 6am-1am CDT/CST

## Technical Implementation Notes

### Website Structure
- **Domain**: courts.mo.gov
- JSP-based website (page.jsp?id=)
- Document files served via file.jsp?id=
- Case.net is a separate application at /casenet/

### Scraping Approach
1. **Opinions by Date**: Parse opinions page by iterating through dates with `?date=MM/DD/YYYY&year=YYYY`
2. **Opinion PDFs**: Direct download from `/file.jsp?id={document_id}`
3. **Multiple Courts**: Filter by `dist=Opinions%20{CourtName}` parameter
4. **Year Selection**: Dropdown with years from 1997 to present

### Date Handling
- Date format in URLs: `MM/DD/YYYY`
- Year parameter separate: `year=YYYY`
- Opinions listed by clickable date buttons on the page
- JavaScript-based date picker, but direct URL access works

### Example API-like Calls
```
# Get all appellate opinions for a specific date
GET https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=12086&dist=Opinions&date=01/16/2026&year=2026

# Get Supreme Court opinions for a specific date
GET https://www.courts.mo.gov/page.jsp?id=12086&dist=Opinions%20Supreme&date=01/13/2026&year=2026

# Download opinion PDF
GET https://www.courts.mo.gov/file.jsp?id=229860
```

## Notes

1. **Existing Scrapers**: Juriscraper already has Missouri scrapers (`mo.py`, `moctapp_eastern.py`, `moctapp_southern.py`, `moctapp_western.py`) in the opinions directory.

2. **Court Terminology**: Only the Chief Justice is referred to as "Justice" - all other judges are "Judge" (not "Justice").

3. **Nonpartisan Court Plan**: Missouri uses a merit-based judicial selection system since 1940 for appellate courts.

4. **Case.net**: The statewide case management system provides public access to case information but has time-of-day restrictions.

5. **Document Access**: Remote access to documents filed before July 1, 2023 requires visiting a courthouse terminal.

6. **Live Streaming**: Supreme Court oral arguments are live streamed via the dockets page.

7. **Briefs**: Briefs filed in Supreme Court cases are available as PDFs on the daily docket pages.

8. **Overview/Summary**: Many opinions have separate "Overview/Summary" documents in addition to the full opinion.
